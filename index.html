About me

Based in Johannesburg, working with US, and UK

I'm an executive virtual assistant for people whose time is the scarcest thing in the business: founders, small leadership teams and executives who are tired of being their own operations department.

I started in enterprise sales at Huawei Technologies through Publicis Commerce. That taught me to follow up without being asked, keep several stakeholders aligned and write messages that get a reply. A BA in Psychology from UNISA helps me read what a situation needs, and a Project Management certificate from Saint Louis University keeps the work organised and on schedule.

I also hold certifications in technology, AI and IT, so I'm comfortable choosing tools, building simple automations and using AI meeting assistants to keep notes accurate. I work in English, isiZulu and Setswana.
